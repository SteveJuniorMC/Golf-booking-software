YourTeeSheet is a digital tee sheet for your pro shop counter. Every tee time for the day on one screen — walk-ups, phone bookings, check-ins, maintenance blocks. It takes about five minutes to set up. It's free, and it stays free.

Built for the counter, not for a computer person

Big type, plain words, and nothing to install. If you can use a paper tee sheet, you can use this one.

The whole day on one screen

Every tee time from first to last, who's on it, and what's open. No tabs, no scrolling sideways.

Book callers weeks out

Click the date, pick the day from a calendar, put them on an open time. Done while they're still on the phone.

Check-in at a glance

One click marks a group checked in. Every status is written in words — not just a color you have to remember.

Block times when you need them

Maintenance, frost delay, league night, an outing — block one time or a whole stretch in one go.

Front and back nine starts

Send groups off the 10th? Turn on back-nine starts and the sheet shows both tees.

Prints clean for the starter

One click prints the day in plain black and white, the way the starter likes it.
