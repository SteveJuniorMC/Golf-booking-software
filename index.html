Suggested 2 minute walkthrough

  1. Open the booking page and book a foursome for tomorrow morning.
  2. Note the confirmation number on the last step.
  3. Open the tee sheet, move to tomorrow, and find that same booking — marked Online.
  4. Add a phone-in booking on an open time, then check a group in.
  5. Block a tee time for maintenance, then go back to the booking page and confirm it is gone.

About this demo

The demo runs entirely in the browser with sample bookings, so it is safe to click anything. Changes are saved in this browser only. Reset demo on the tee sheet restores the starting data before your next walkthrough.

Course name, rates, hours, and tee time intervals are all configurable — in this demo they live at the top of assets/data.js.
